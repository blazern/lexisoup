Lexisoup is a "soup" of lexical data about foreign words:
- Words' translations
- Explanations
- Forms
- Synonyms
- Usages examples

Lexisoup has no data of its own.
Instead, in the hope of bringing a better understanding of the word, lexisoup brings together data from the following sources:
- PanLex
- Kaikki (Wiktionary)
- Tatoeba
- Wortschatz Leipzig
- ChatGPT

It is a small open source app, its source code can be found here: https://github.com/blazern/lexisoup/
